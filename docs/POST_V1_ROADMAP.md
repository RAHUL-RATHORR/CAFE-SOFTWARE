# DineFlow Post-v1.0.0 Product Roadmap

The following architectural and business capabilities are intentionally scheduled for future release cycles (v1.1+) to maintain the stability and scope freeze of Version 1.0.0.

---

## 1. Advanced CRM & Customer Loyalty (v1.1)
- **Points & Rewards Engine**: Spend-based loyalty points accumulation, tier levels (Bronze, Silver, Gold), and redemption at POS.
- **Customer Segmentation**: Automatic categorization of customers based on RFM metrics (Recency, Frequency, Monetary value).
- **Targeted WhatsApp & SMS Campaigns**: Filter-driven automated promotional broadcasts with coupon codes.

---

## 2. Supplier Procurement & Purchase Workflows (v1.2)
- **Vendor Portal**: Direct vendor access to receive Purchase Orders (POs) and submit delivery manifests.
- **Automated Reorder Triggering**: Dynamic PO generation when ingredient stock falls below safety buffer thresholds.
- **Three-Way Matching**: Automated reconciliation of Purchase Orders, Goods Received Notes (GRN), and Vendor Invoices.

---

## 3. Advanced Accounting & Enterprise Integrations (v1.3)
- **General Ledger Sync**: Two-way integration with QuickBooks, Tally Prime, and Zoho Books for automated journal entries.
- **Multi-Entity Taxation**: Support for multi-national tax regimens (VAT, Sales Tax, Harmonized Sales Tax) beyond Indian GST.
- **Cost Center Allocation**: Multi-branch expense allocation and centralized corporate treasury reporting.

---

## 4. AI-Driven Forecasting & Menu Engineering (v1.4)
- **Predictive Demand Forecasting**: Machine learning models predicting hourly prep volume based on day of week, weather, and seasonal trends.
- **Smart Wastage Prevention**: Recommendations on prep batch sizes to minimize perishable ingredient spoilage.
- **Menu Engineering Matrix**: Automated BCG-matrix classification of menu dishes (Stars, Plowhorses, Puzzles, Dogs) based on velocity and margin.

---

## 5. Native Hardware & Offline-First POS (v2.0)
- **Native Android & iOS POS Apps**: Dedicated tablet applications with offline local SQLite storage and peer-to-peer LAN sync during internet outages.
- **Integrated Payment Terminals**: Direct serial/USB protocol integration with EMV card readers (Stripe Terminal, Pine Labs, Paytm EDC).
