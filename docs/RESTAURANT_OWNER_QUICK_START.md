# DineFlow Restaurant Owner Quick Start Guide

Welcome to DineFlow! This 14-step guide walks you through setting up your restaurant and taking your first live orders.

---

### Step 1: Login
- Navigate to your DineFlow instance URL (e.g. `https://app.dineflow.com/login`).
- Enter your Owner email and initial temporary password provided in your activation email.
- You will be prompted to set a new, secure password upon your first login.

### Step 2: Set Up Restaurant Profile
- Go to **Settings ➔ Restaurant Profile**.
- Upload your restaurant logo, set your legal entity name, default currency (e.g. `INR`, `USD`), and contact details.

### Step 3: Add Your Branch
- Go to **Branches** and click **Add New Branch**.
- Enter the branch name, physical address, phone number, and operating hours.

### Step 4: Configure Tables
- In the branch view, go to **Tables**.
- Add your dining tables (e.g. Table 1, Table 2, Outdoor A, Bar 1) with seating capacity.

### Step 5: Generate & Print Table QR Codes
- Select all tables and click **Generate QR Codes**.
- Download the high-resolution printable PDF sheets containing your branded QR codes.
- Laminate or place the QR cards on each physical dining table.

### Step 6: Create Categories & Menu Items
- Navigate to **Menu ➔ Categories** to create sections (e.g. *Appetizers*, *Main Course*, *Beverages*).
- Add **Menu Items** with names, descriptions, base prices, tax rates (GST 5% / 18%), and food types (Veg / Non-Veg / Vegan).
- Add item variants (e.g. *Small / Medium / Large*) and optional add-ons if needed.

### Step 7: Set Up Raw Ingredients (Optional for Inventory)
- Go to **Inventory ➔ Ingredients**.
- Add your core ingredients (e.g. *Milk*, *Coffee Beans*, *Flour*, *Cooking Oil*) with unit of measurement and initial stock levels.

### Step 8: Link Recipes to Menu Items
- Open **Inventory ➔ Recipes** or edit a menu item to define its recipe BOM.
- Specify ingredient quantities (e.g. 1 Espresso uses 18g Coffee Beans, 150ml Milk) to enable automatic stock deduction.

### Step 9: Configure Billing & Taxes
- Go to **Settings ➔ Billing & GST**.
- Enter your GSTIN / Tax Identification Number, legal registered name, and choose whether prices include or exclude tax.

### Step 10: Configure Thermal Printers
- In **Settings ➔ Printers**, add your receipt and KOT printers.
- Select your printer type (Network / USB / System Dialog) and paper width (80mm or 58mm).

### Step 11: Add Staff Members
- Go to **Staff Management ➔ Add Employee**.
- Create user accounts for your Managers, Cashiers, and Kitchen Staff with appropriate role permissions.

### Step 12: Start Taking Orders on POS
- Open the **POS** view (`/billing/pos`).
- Choose Dine-in or Takeaway, tap menu items to add to cart, select table number, and click **Place Order / Send to Kitchen**.

### Step 13: Monitor Orders in Kitchen (KDS)
- Kitchen tablets or display screens open `/kitchen`.
- Cooks see tickets in real-time, tap **Start Preparing**, and tap **Ready** once dishes are plated.

### Step 14: Daily Closing & Reports
- At the end of each shift or business day, go to **Administration ➔ Daily Closing**.
- Enter the physical cash counted in the register.
- Review total sales, card/UPI reconciliations, and lock the day.
- View or export your daily sales summary report in PDF or Excel.
