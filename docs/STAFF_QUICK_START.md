# DineFlow Staff Quick Start Guide

A practical workflow guide for frontline Cashiers, Waiters, and Kitchen Staff.

---

## 1. Cashier & Counter Operations

### Start of Shift
1. Log in at `https://app.dineflow.com/login` with your staff credentials.
2. Ensure your register drawer has the designated float cash.

### Taking a POS Order
1. Click **POS** in the main navigation.
2. Select **Order Type**:
   - **Dine-In**: Select Table Number from the floor layout.
   - **Takeaway / Delivery**: Enter Customer Name / Mobile (optional).
3. Tap menu items to add to cart.
   - Use search or category tabs for quick navigation.
   - Tap an item to customize variants or add-ons.
4. Click **Send to KDS / Place Order** (sends KOT to kitchen immediately).

### Settling Bills & Accepting Payments
1. Open the table or order in the **POS / Billing** screen.
2. Click **Checkout / Settle Bill**.
3. Choose Payment Method:
   - **Cash**: Enter received amount; the screen calculates change due.
   - **UPI / QR**: Ask customer to scan your dynamic counter QR; tap confirm once payment is received.
   - **Card**: Swipe or tap card on POS terminal; enter approval code.
   - **Split Payment**: Split between multiple tenders if requested.
4. Click **Complete & Print Receipt**. The thermal printer automatically generates the finalized GST invoice.

---

## 2. Kitchen Display System (KDS) Operations

### Kitchen Screen Workflow
1. Open the tablet / monitor at `/kitchen`.
2. As new tickets arrive, an audible chime sounds and the order appears under **Pending / Received**.
3. Tap **Start Preparing** when the station begins cooking (ticket status changes to orange `Preparing`).
4. You can cross off individual items as they are finished by tapping the item row.
5. Tap **Order Ready** when all items are plated. The waiter/server is notified that food is ready for pickup.
6. Once collected, tap **Served / Complete** to archive the ticket.

---

## 3. End of Shift / Closing
1. Print the X-Reading / shift summary from the POS screen.
2. Count all cash banknotes and coins in the drawer.
3. Hand drawer totals to the Shift Manager or complete the Daily Closing reconciliation form.
4. Log out of your session on the shared terminal.
